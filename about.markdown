<h1>About Me</h1>

<div style="display: flex; gap: 2rem; align-items: flex-start; flex-wrap: wrap; margin-bottom: 2rem;">
  <div style="flex: 1; min-width: 300px;">
    <p class="hero-text">
      <strong>Dr. Eddwi Hesky Hasdeo</strong> is a Senior Researcher (Peneliti Ahli Madya) at the Research Center for Quantum Physics, National Research and Innovation Agency (BRIN), Indonesia.
    </p>
    <p>
      His research centers on theoretical condensed matter physics, focusing on the fundamental roles of the electronic wave function geometry (quantum metric and Berry curvature), hydrodynamic transport of viscous electron fluids, topological photonics, and thermoelectric power enhancement in low-dimensional and nanoscale materials.
    </p>
    <p>
      Prior to joining BRIN, Dr. Hasdeo was a Research Scientist in the group of Prof. Thomas Schmidt at the University of Luxembourg (2020–2024), where he served as Principal Investigator for an FNR CORE Junior project. He was also a Research Scientist at IHPC, A*STAR, Singapore (2017–2018), and a postdoctoral researcher at Tohoku University, Japan (2016). He received his B.Sc. in Physics as valedictorian from Brawijaya University, Indonesia (2011), and earned both his M.Sc. (2013) and Ph.D. (2016) in Physics from Tohoku University under the mentorship of Prof. Riichiro Saito.
    </p>
    <p>
      He has authored more than 45 publications in prestigious journals including <em>Physical Review Letters</em>, <em>Physical Review B</em>, <em>Nano Letters</em>, <em>ACS Nano</em>, and <em>Applied Physics Letters</em>, receiving over 1,745 citations (H-index 19).
    </p>
