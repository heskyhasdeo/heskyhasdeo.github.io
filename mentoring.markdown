Many students who began research under my supervision have published peer-reviewed journal papers (in <em>PRB</em>, <em>JAP</em>, <em>AIP Conf. Proc.</em>, etc.) and earned competitive scholarships for Ph.D. and postdoc positions at top international institutions including <strong>UC Berkeley</strong>, the <strong>University of Cambridge</strong>, <strong>KAIST</strong>, <strong>City University of Hong Kong</strong>, <strong>Tokyo University of Agriculture and Technology</strong>, <strong>Osaka University</strong>, and <strong>Shanghai Jiao Tong University</strong>.
