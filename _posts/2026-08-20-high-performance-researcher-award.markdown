<h1>Awarded High Performance Researcher 2026</h1>
<p class="hero-affiliation">August 20, 2026 &bull; News & Awards</p>

<p>
  I am honored to receive the <strong>Peneliti Berkinerja Tinggi (High Performance Researcher)</strong> award from the Research Organization of Nanotechnology and Materials (ORNM), National Research and Innovation Agency (BRIN) for 2026.
</p>
