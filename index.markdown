<h2>Research Highlights</h2>
<p>
  My research bridges quantum materials, photonics, and hydrodynamic transport to understand non-classical driving forces in solids and discover platforms for future quantum technologies:
</p>

<div class="grid-2">
  <div class="card">
    <h3 class="card-title"><a href="{{ '/research/#hydrodynamics' | relative_url }}">&#127754; Hydrodynamic Electron Transport</a></h3>
    <p>
      Developing Navier-Stokes equations for electrons in clean 2D materials. Discovered Coulomb drag viscosity, negative drag conductivity in viscous channels (<em>Phys. Rev. B 2023</em>), and quantum geometric Berry curvature corrections to electronic flow (<em>Phys. Rev. B 2021</em>).
    </p>
    <a href="{{ '/research/#hydrodynamics' | relative_url }}">Read topic overview &rarr;</a>
  </div>

  <div class="card">
    <h3 class="card-title"><a href="{{ '/research/#quantum-geometry' | relative_url }}">&#9883; Quantum Geometry & Optoelectronics</a></h3>
    <p>
      Investigating how the internal structure of the electronic wave function (quantum metric and Berry curvature) dictates cyclotron motion without magnetic fields (<em>New J. Phys. 2019</em>) and optical rotations in topological flat bands (<em>New J. Phys. 2022</em>).
    </p>
    <a href="{{ '/research/#quantum-geometry' | relative_url }}">Read topic overview &rarr;</a>
  </div>

  <div class="card">
    <h3 class="card-title"><a href="{{ '/research/#topological-optics' | relative_url }}">&#128187; Topological Optics & Qubit Entanglement</a></h3>
    <p>
      Demonstrating topological surface waves (unidirectional Surface Plasmon Polaritons) in Weyl semimetals. Utilizing unidirectional SPPs to mediate long-lived entanglement between quantum bits (qubits) for quantum architectures (<em>Phys. Rev. B 2022</em>).
    </p>
    <a href="{{ '/research/#topological-optics' | relative_url }}">Read topic overview &rarr;</a>
  </div>

  <div class="card">
    <h3 class="card-title"><a href="{{ '/research/#thermoelectrics' | relative_url }}">&#9889; Thermoelectric Transport in Nanostructures</a></h3>
    <p>
      Formulating the universal quantum confinement rule ($L / \Lambda < 1$) for enhanced thermoelectric power factors in nanostructures (<em>Phys. Rev. Lett. 2016</em>) and optimizing bandgaps in Dirac materials (<em>J. Appl. Phys. 2019</em>).
    </p>
    <a href="{{ '/research/#thermoelectrics' | relative_url }}">Read topic overview &rarr;</a>
  </div>
</div>
